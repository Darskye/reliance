      ISEA NAVIGATOR · MISSION BRIEF
      RELIANCE
      “Moving forward, together.”
      
        
      
        
      Years after a war over energy, the world lives in the Dimming. Light, water, power and food are rationed, and every watt counts. The International Space Exploration Agency has one answer left: look up.
      
        
      Commander Roman Reznov, Science Officer Dr. Catherine Gray and Flight Engineer John Winchester are chosen to crew the Helios, a three-person deep space survey vessel, on a nine-month, twenty-two-trillion-kilometre search for Ichor: a theorized energy source that could give humanity a fresh start on a new world.
      
        
      Out there, they will have to rely on the ship, on each other, and on the agency's thinking machine, Talos.
      
        
      
          
          
          
        
      
        
      RELIANCE is in pre-production: casting, crewing up and crowdfunding.
      
      
      

      
      
        
      The world of RELIANCE
      
        
      Agency reference files. Some entries are restricted.
      
        
      
          
      The Dimming
      The age of scarcity that followed a war over energy and resources. Light, water, power and food are all rationed.
      
          
      The Allocation Bureau
      Runs the rationing. Its public information films remind everyone to report waste. Every watt counts.
      
          
      The ISEA
      The International Space Exploration Agency, searching the stars for a new source of power. Motto: moving forward, together.
      
          
      The Helios
      Hull RV-2719. A deep space survey vessel with a crew of three, hyperspace capable. 
      
          
      Ichor
      A theorized energy source. If it is real, it could give humanity a fresh start on a new world.
      
          
      Talos
      The agency's thinking machine, built by the Talos Program under software engineer Kim Seo-yeon. FILE RESTRICTED 
      
          
      Kotoshima Technologies
      Makers of the CyberCuff, the wrist computer worn by every ISEA crew member.
      
          
      Cube Meal
      Four meals in one cube, and proud sponsor of the ISEA. Be there and be square!
